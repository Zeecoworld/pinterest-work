Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Who we are

Zeecomedia Pins ("the App", "we", "us") is a content scheduling tool operated by Zeecomedia Tech Enterprises, based in Lagos, Nigeria. It helps us create, schedule and publish Pinterest content for our own brand and for clients who authorise us to do so.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: username and password (stored hashed) for people who log in to the dashboard.
  • Pinterest connection data: when you connect a Pinterest account, we receive an access token and basic account and board information (such as board names and IDs) through the Pinterest API.
  • Content you create: pin titles, descriptions, destination links, categories, schedules and images you upload or generate in the App.
  • Technical data: standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and request times, used for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment card information, and we do not read your private Pinterest messages.

3. How we use information

  • To create and publish pins and manage boards on your behalf, only as you or your team instruct.
  • To schedule and automate posting, and to show status and history in the dashboard.
  • To secure the App, prevent abuse and fix errors.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Pinterest data for advertising or to build profiles of individuals.

4. Services that process data for us

  • Pinterest: receives the content we publish through its API, subject to Pinterest's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Supabase: hosts our database and, where enabled, stores pin images.
  • Render: hosts the application.
  • AI image and text tools: where enabled, pin prompts or titles may be sent to an image generation service (a self-hosted Stable Diffusion server or the Google Gemini API) to produce or edit images.

These providers process data only to deliver their services to us.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep account and content data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can disconnect your Pinterest account at any time, which stops all access, and you can revoke access from your Pinterest settings. To have your data deleted, email us at the address below and we will act on the request within a reasonable time.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords and access-restricted credentials, and we limit dashboard access to authorised users. No online system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, and GDPR where it applies), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children

The App is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
